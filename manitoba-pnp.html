曼省省提名
Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program

Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program（MPNP） 是曼尼托巴省根据本省经济发展、 劳动力市场和社区需要， 选择符合条件的技术工人、 国际毕业生及商业投资申请人的 省提名移民项目。

对已经在Manitoba工作、 在当地完成学业、 与Manitoba具有稳定联系， 或拥有当地雇主及省级招聘支持的申请人而言， MPNP可能成为取得加拿大永久居民身份的重要途径。

The Manitoba Provincial Nominee Program allows Manitoba to nominate eligible skilled workers, international graduates, entrepreneurs and investors who can contribute to the province’s economy, labour market and communities. Eligibility and competitiveness may depend on employment, occupation, education, language ability, Manitoba connections, EOI ranking and current provincial priorities.

什么是Manitoba PNP？ | What Is the MPNP?

MPNP是一个以经济移民为核心的省提名项目， 主要目的是选择能够在Manitoba 实现经济建立并满足当地劳动力市场 和社区发展需要的申请人。

目前MPNP主要包括三大类别： Skilled Worker Stream、 International Education Stream 以及Business Investor Stream。

Skilled Worker Stream主要面向 在Manitoba工作或与Manitoba具有合资格联系的技术工人； International Education Stream 面向符合条件的Manitoba国际毕业生； Business Investor Stream 则主要面向计划在Manitoba经营企业 或投资农场的商业申请人。

For most Skilled Worker and International Education pathways, the MPNP uses an Expression of Interest system. An EOI is not an immigration application. Candidates create a profile, receive a ranking score, and may later receive a Letter of Advice to Apply if selected by Manitoba.

Manitoba PNP主要项目 | Key MPNP Pathways

Manitoba省提名的不同Stream 对工作地点、Manitoba联系、 毕业学校、职业、语言、 工作经验及商业背景有不同要求。

Skilled Worker

曼省境内技术工人
Skilled Worker in Manitoba

Skilled Worker in Manitoba is designed for eligible foreign workers who have established employment and a strong connection to Manitoba.

该类别主要适用于已经在Manitoba工作、 并与当地劳动力市场建立实际联系的外国工人。 工作经验、雇主、长期工作机会及申请人的身份情况 都需要结合具体项目要求评估。

Skilled Worker Overseas

海外技术工人
Skilled Worker Overseas

Skilled Worker Overseas is intended for internationally trained and experienced workers who can demonstrate an established connection to Manitoba.

申请人通常需要证明与Manitoba存在合资格联系， 例如Manitoba Support、 过去在省内学习或工作的Manitoba Experience， 或通过Strategic Recruitment Initiative 获得Manitoba Invitation。

International Education

职业就业通道
Career Employment Pathway

The Career Employment Pathway provides a faster nomination pathway for eligible Manitoba graduates who obtain long-term employment in occupations connected to their education and provincial labour market needs.

适用于符合要求的Manitoba毕业生， 特别是毕业后获得与所学专业相关、 并符合Manitoba劳动力市场需求 的长期工作机会的申请人。

International Education

研究生实习通道
Graduate Internship Pathway

The Graduate Internship Pathway is available to certain Manitoba master’s and doctoral graduates who meet applicable internship and program requirements.

该通道主要针对符合条件的 Manitoba硕士及博士毕业生。 是否符合资格需要结合学历、 实习或研究背景及具体项目条件进行判断。

International Education

国际学生企业家试点
International Student Entrepreneur Pilot

The International Student Entrepreneur Pilot provides certain Manitoba graduates with an opportunity to pursue entrepreneurship rather than traditional employment.

该试点适用于部分符合条件的 Manitoba国际毕业生， 允许申请人通过实际经营企业 而不是传统就业路径争取省提名。

Strategic Recruitment

战略招聘与定向邀请
Strategic Recruitment Initiatives

Manitoba may identify and invite qualified candidates through Strategic Recruitment Initiatives in Manitoba or overseas.

Manitoba会根据特定劳动力市场需求， 通过Strategic Recruitment Initiative 对部分候选人进行定向招聘和邀请。 这类机会与省级职业需求及招聘活动密切相关。

Business Investor

企业家通道
Entrepreneur Pathway

The Entrepreneur Pathway is intended for experienced business owners and senior managers who intend to establish or purchase a business in Manitoba.

适合具有企业经营或高级管理经验， 并计划在Manitoba创办、 收购及实际经营企业的申请人。 评估时需要结合商业经验、 净资产、语言、投资计划及商业方案。

Business Investor

农场投资通道
Farm Investor Pathway

The Farm Investor Pathway is intended for qualified applicants who intend to establish and actively operate a farm business in rural Manitoba.

该通道主要面向具有相关农业经营背景， 并计划在Manitoba农村地区 建立和实际经营农场企业的申请人。

Manitoba PNP不能只看EOI分数

MPNP虽然采用EOI排名系统， 但实际邀请并不意味着 每一轮都只是简单地按照总分 从高到低普遍邀请。

Manitoba可能根据劳动力市场需要， 对特定职业、行业、法语申请人、 Strategic Recruitment候选人 或其他符合省级经济重点的申请人 进行定向选择。

因此， 一个申请人的EOI分数很重要， 但职业、Manitoba联系、 当地就业、语言能力及 是否符合当前定向邀请重点 同样值得评估。

Manitoba PNP competitiveness should therefore be assessed by looking at both EOI ranking and the applicant’s occupation, Manitoba connection, employment background, language ability, education and current provincial selection priorities.
