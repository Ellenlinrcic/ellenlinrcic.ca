加拿大省提名申请
Provincial Nominee Programs

加拿大各省根据当地劳动力需求、经济发展目标和人口规划， 设置不同的省提名移民项目。申请人应结合自身教育背景、 工作经验、语言能力、雇主支持情况及居住意向， 评估适合自己的移民方向。

Canadian provinces operate immigration programs designed to address regional labour needs, economic priorities, and population growth. The appropriate pathway depends on an applicant’s education, work experience, language ability, employer support, and genuine intention to settle in the relevant province.

省提名申请如何评估？ | How We Assess Provincial Pathways

合适的省提名项目，不只是看分数或职业名称， 更需要结合申请人的整体情况进行判断。

常见评估因素 | Key Assessment Factors

  • 目前所在地及加拿大身份 | Current location and immigration status
  • 教育背景及专业方向 | Education and field of study
  • 加拿大或海外工作经验 | Canadian and international work experience
  • 英语或法语能力 | English or French language ability
  • 加拿大雇主及工作机会 | Canadian employer and job opportunities
  • 工作地点、薪资及岗位职责 | Work location, wages, and job duties
  • 与所在省份的联系及定居意向 | Provincial ties and settlement intention

我可以提供的服务 | Available Services

  • 初步资格评估 | Initial eligibility assessment
  • 比较不同省份的申请方向 | Comparison of possible provincial pathways
  • 评估雇主及工作机会 | Employer and employment assessment
  • 分析申请风险与潜在障碍 | Identification of risks and potential concerns
  • 制定工作、身份与移民衔接计划 | Work permit and immigration pathway planning
  • 整理申请文件并准备说明材料 | Document preparation and supporting submissions
  • 省提名及后续永久居民申请 | Provincial nomination and permanent residence applications
