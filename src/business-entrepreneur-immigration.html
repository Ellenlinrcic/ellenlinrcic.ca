从移民资格评估到企业落地的整体规划
Planning From Immigration Eligibility to Business Establishment

Canadian business immigration is not a single program. Available pathways may involve a provincial entrepreneur stream, a corporate expansion project, a temporary work permit strategy, or the management of an application already in process. Eligibility depends on the applicant’s background, source of funds, proposed investment, ownership structure, business location, and ability to actively operate the business.

加拿大商业移民并不是一个单一项目。可考虑的途径可能涉及省提名企业家类别、 企业在加拿大的拓展项目、临时工签安排，或已经递交案件的后续管理。 申请资格取决于申请人的背景、资金来源、拟投资额、股权结构、经营地点及实际管理能力。

商业移民需要注意的事项
Important Considerations

Business immigration combines immigration eligibility with real commercial activity. Applicants should obtain independent legal, accounting, tax, and business advice where required.

商业移民同时涉及移民资格与真实商业活动。申请人应当根据需要另行取得法律、会计、税务及商业意见。

  • Buying a business, incorporating a company, or owning property does not by itself create immigration status. | 收购企业、注册公司或持有物业，本身并不会自动产生加拿大移民身份。
  • Many entrepreneur streams require active management and completion of a performance agreement before nomination. | 许多企业家项目要求申请人主动经营，并在获得提名前完成绩效协议。
  • Net worth and investment funds must be supported by credible evidence of lawful accumulation and availability. | 净资产及投资资金应有可信证据证明其合法积累过程及可用性。
  • A temporary work permit or provincial approval does not automatically guarantee permanent residence. | 临时工签或省级阶段获批，并不当然保证最终取得永久居民身份。
  • The federal Start-Up Visa and Self-Employed Persons programs are currently paused for new applications. | 联邦 Start-Up Visa 与自雇移民项目目前均暂停接收新申请。
