纽芬兰与拉布拉多省发出97份省提名邀请： 9月4日最新一轮NLPNP抽选

Read in English

纽芬兰与拉布拉多省 （Newfoundland and Labrador） 近日公布最新一轮经济移民邀请结果。

2026年9月4日， 该省通过Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Nominee Program（NLPNP） 共发出97份邀请。

与上一轮不同， 本轮公布的97份邀请全部属于NLPNP， 没有公布Atlantic Immigration Program（AIP） 的申请选择。

本轮公布了哪些信息？

项目 本轮结果
抽选日期 2026年9月4日
NLPNP邀请 97份
AIP选择 本轮没有公布
最低分数 未公布
职业类别 未公布
工资门槛 未公布
申请人所在地 未公布

由于官方没有公布最低分数、 职业类别、工资门槛 或申请人所在地， 不能仅凭这97份邀请 判断国际毕业生、境外申请人 或某类职业的条件已经放宽。

与上一轮相比有什么变化？

在此前的2026年8月26日一轮中， 纽芬兰与拉布拉多省共进行了 140份经济移民申请选择。

  • NLPNP： 94份；
  • AIP： 46份。

而9月4日这一轮， 只有NLPNP的97份邀请， 没有同时公布AIP选择。

这说明纽芬兰与拉布拉多省 仍然持续通过NLPNP 邀请符合条件的申请人。

但由于官方没有进一步公布 被邀请申请人的具体背景， 不能把单次邀请数量变化 理解为项目资格或选择标准已经放宽。

对申请人有什么实际意义？

对于已经向纽芬兰与拉布拉多省提交EOI、 目前在当地工作， 或已经获得当地雇主支持的申请人， 近期应该特别留意自己的 申请账户和电子邮箱。

如果已经收到邀请， 应尽快确认：

  • 邀请适用的具体申请类别；
  • 正式申请的递交期限；
  • 雇主支持是否仍然有效；
  • 工作许可及加拿大身份是否符合要求；
  • 工作、工资及其他资格条件 是否在正式递交时仍然满足。

没有收到邀请怎么办？

对于尚未收到邀请的申请人， 没有必要因为本轮发出97份邀请， 就立即判断自己的机会明显增加 或明显下降。

由于本轮没有公布最低分数、 职业或申请人所在地等详细信息， 实际机会仍然需要结合 申请人的具体背景进行判断。

真正影响NLPNP申请机会的， 仍然是申请人适用的具体类别、 当地就业情况、雇主支持、 加拿大身份以及省政府当时的 移民优先方向。

Newfoundland and Labrador Issues 97 Invitations in Latest NLPNP Round

Newfoundland and Labrador has announced the results of its latest provincial immigration invitation round.

On September 4, 2026, the province issued 97 invitations, all through the Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Nominee Program (NLPNP).

Unlike the previous round, no application selections under the Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP) were reported.

What Do We Know About This Round?

  • Date: September 4, 2026;
  • NLPNP invitations: 97;
  • AIP selections: None reported;
  • Minimum score: Not disclosed;
  • Occupations targeted: Not disclosed;
  • Wage threshold: Not disclosed;
  • Applicant location: Not disclosed.

Because these details were not published, the 97 invitations should not be interpreted as evidence that Newfoundland and Labrador has relaxed its requirements for international graduates, overseas applicants or applicants in any particular occupation.

How Does This Compare with the Previous Round?

The province's previous selection round took place on August 26, 2026, when a total of 140 applications were selected:

  • 94 through the NLPNP;
  • 46 through the AIP.

The September 4 round therefore represents another selection under the NLPNP, but this time without any reported AIP selections.

The results confirm that Newfoundland and Labrador continues to invite candidates through its provincial nominee program.

However, without further information about the candidates selected, it is too early to draw conclusions about changes in the province's immigration priorities.

What Should Applicants Do?

Applicants who have already submitted an Expression of Interest, are currently working in Newfoundland and Labrador, or have support from a local employer should check their accounts and email carefully for an invitation.

Those who receive an invitation should promptly review:

  • the applicable submission deadline;
  • immigration status;
  • employer support;
  • employment conditions; and
  • other program requirements.

For applicants who were not invited, the latest round does not necessarily indicate that their chances have increased or decreased.

Eligibility and selection prospects continue to depend on the applicant's specific immigration stream, employment situation, employer support, legal status in Canada, and the province's current immigration priorities.

已经提交NLPNP EOI？

如果您已经提交EOI、 正在纽芬兰与拉布拉多省工作， 或已经获得当地雇主支持， 可以检查自己的账户及邮箱 是否收到省政府邀请。

尚未收到邀请的申请人， 也可以结合自己的职业、 雇主、工作情况、加拿大身份 及适用类别重新评估申请机会。
