# 配偶担保遇到障碍，H&C应当如何提出？

<p><strong>When Spousal Sponsorship Faces Obstacles: How Should H&amp;C Relief Be Requested?</strong></p>

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<p>处理复杂配偶担保案件时，申请人常会问：“如果普通担保条件不符合，可不可以请移民官考虑人道主义及同情理由（H&amp;C）？”</p>

<p>需要先弄清楚的，是<strong>通过什么程序提出请求，以及希望豁免哪一项具体要求</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<h2><strong>独立</strong><strong>H&amp;C</strong><strong>申请，与担保中的豁免请求，需要分别分</strong><strong>析</strong></h2>

<p>IRCC于2026年10月6日小幅更新了连续及并行H&amp;C申请的处理指引。这不是新的移民项目，也不能解读为H&amp;C条件全面放宽。</p>

<p>指引说明，当独立H&amp;C申请与境内配偶担保申请同时存在时，独立H&amp;C申请通常会被暂缓。如果配偶担保最终被拒，尚未完成的H&amp;C申请可按程序恢复处理。</p>

<p>但这一安排不能直接套用于担保申请内部提出的H&amp;C请求。指引另有关于并行H&amp;C请求的限制，必须结合相关受理规则及具体请求性质分析。</p>

<h2><strong>困难的经历，需要对应明确的请求和证</strong><strong>据</strong></h2>

<p>对于涉及未申报家庭成员、申请类别资格或既往拒签记录的案件，仅仅写“希望获得人道主义考虑”往往不够。</p>

<p>准备申请时，需要明确：</p>

<ul>

<li>哪一项规定构成申请障碍；</li>

<li>请求豁免的具体要求及法律依据；</li>

<li>家庭关系、子女利益和个人困难有哪些证据支持；</li>

<li>本次陈述是否与此前申请记录一致。</li>

</ul>

<p>如果此前已有H&amp;C评估，再次申请还应说明新增情况及证据。IRCC可以参考过去的资料和认定，也必须考虑本次提交的新信息。</p>

<p><strong>复杂案件的关键，不只是增加一封解释信，而是把法律障碍、程序选择和证据联系起来</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<p>如果您的配偶担保收到程序公正信、曾被拒签，或涉及未申报家庭成员问题，欢迎联系我们评估下一步方案。</p>

<p>本文为一般信息，不构成针对个案的法律意见。</p>

<h2><strong>When Spousal Sponsorship Faces Obstacles: How Should H&amp;C Relief Be Requested?</strong></h2>

<p>In complex spousal sponsorship cases, applicants often ask: “If I cannot meet the regular requirements, can an officer consider humanitarian and compassionate (H&amp;C) grounds?”</p>

<p>The first questions are <strong>how the request should be </strong><strong>made</strong><strong> and which specific requirement needs to be waived.</strong></p>

<h3><strong>Different Procedures Require Different Analysis</strong></h3>

<p>On October 6, 2026, IRCC made minor updates to its instructions on consecutive and concurrent H&amp;C applications. This was not a new immigration program or an announcement of broadly relaxed eligibility requirements.</p>

<p>The instructions explain that a standalone H&amp;C application will generally be held in abeyance while an inland spousal sponsorship application is pending. If the sponsorship application is ultimately refused, processing of the outstanding H&amp;C application may resume under standard procedures.</p>

<p>This arrangement should not automatically be applied to an H&amp;C request made within a sponsorship application. The instructions separately address restrictions on concurrent H&amp;C requests. The applicable intake rules and the nature of the requested relief require careful examination.</p>

<h3><strong>Personal Hardship Must Be Connected to a Clear Request and Supporting Evidence</strong></h3>

<p>For cases involving undeclared family members, class eligibility or previous refusals, simply asking for “humanitarian consideration” may not adequately explain the relief sought.</p>

<p>A carefully prepared submission should identify:</p>

<ul>

<li>The provision creating the obstacle;</li>

<li>The specific exemption requested and its legal basis;</li>

<li>Evidence of family relationships, the best interests of affected children and personal hardship;</li>

<li>Whether the submissions are consistent with previous immigration records.</li>

</ul>

<p>Where an applicant has already received an H&amp;C assessment, a subsequent application should explain any new circumstances and evidence. IRCC may consider earlier information and findings, but must also consider new information submitted with the latest application.</p>

<p><strong>A complex case requires more than an additional explanation letter. The legal obstacle, procedural approach and supporting evidence must work together.</strong></p>
