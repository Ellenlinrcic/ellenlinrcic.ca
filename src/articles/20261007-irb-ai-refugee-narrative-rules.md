# 难民陈述可以用AI代写吗？IRB已明确限制

<p><strong>Can AI Write Your Refugee Narrative? IRB Rules Set Clear Limits</strong></p>

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<p>准备难民申请或移民上诉材料时，有些人会让AI把个人经历“写得更完整、更有说服力”。但涉及提交给加拿大移民及难民委员会（IRB）的个人证据，这种做法可能违反规定。</p>

<p>IRB关于人工智能使用的实务通知已于 2026年9月7日生效，适用于其全部四个审理部门，包括难民保护、难民上诉、移民审理及移民上诉程序。</p>

<h2>个人经历不能交给AI生成或实质改写</h2>

<p>通知禁止使用AI起草难民申请理由陈述（BOC narrative）、宣誓书或证人声明，也禁止用AI改写个人故事，或制作、修改照片、视频、截图等佐证材料。</p>

<p>拼写检查、轻微语法修正和格式整理仍可使用，但不能生成证据或实质改变内容。判断重点是工具实际做了什么，而不是把操作称为“润色”还是“修改”。</p>

<h2>AI翻译也需要披露</h2>

<p>使用AI翻译或转录必须披露。翻译文件仍须符合人工译者声明要求；其他允许使用AI生成或实质修改的提交材料，也须按通知附上声明并核实内容。披露AI使用，并不能使原本被禁止的个人证据代写变得合规。</p>

<h2>申请提醒</h2>

<p>违反规定可能导致文件不被接纳、证据不被采信或可信度受到负面评价；代理人还可能面临监管转介或出庭限制。</p>

<p>这份通知针对IRB程序，不应扩大解释为所有IRCC签证及移民申请一律禁止使用AI。准备材料前，应先确认文件用途和适用要求。</p>

<p>个人证据应忠实反映本人所知、所见及所经历的事实。语言更流畅，不能代替真实性。</p>

<h2>Can AI Write Your Refugee Narrative? IRB Rules Set Clear Limits</h2>

<p>Applicants sometimes use AI to make personal accounts sound more complete or persuasive. For personal evidence submitted to Canada’s Immigration and Refugee Board (IRB), this may breach its rules.</p>

<p>Effective September 7, 2026, the IRB’s AI practice notice applies across all four divisions, covering refugee claims, refugee appeals, immigration hearings and immigration appeals.</p>

<h3>Personal Evidence Must Not Be AI-Generated</h3>

<p>AI must not draft or materially rewrite Basis of Claim narratives, affidavits or witness statements, or create or alter supporting photographs, videos or screenshots.</p>

<p>Minor spelling, grammar and formatting assistance remains permitted if it does not generate evidence or materially change it.</p>

<h3>Translation Requires Disclosure</h3>

<p>AI-assisted translation and transcription must always be disclosed. Translated documents still require a human translator’s declaration. Other permitted AI-generated or materially changed submissions require the prescribed declaration and verification.</p>

<p>Disclosure does not make prohibited AI drafting of personal evidence permissible.</p>

<h3>Why This Matters</h3>

<p>Non-compliance may affect document acceptance and credibility. Counsel may face regulatory referrals or restrictions on appearing before the IRB.</p>

<p>This notice governs IRB proceedings; it is not a blanket AI prohibition for every IRCC application.</p>

<p>Personal evidence should faithfully reflect the individual’s knowledge and experience. Polished language cannot replace authenticity.</p>
