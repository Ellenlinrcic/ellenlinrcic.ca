<div id="chinese-version" lang="zh-Hans">
<p>安大略省省提名计划（OINP）近日调整了Ontario Workforce Priority Stream的意向表达（Expression of Interest，简称EOI）评分标准，提高了高收入申请人在加拿大工作收入记录方面的评分。</p>

<p>新规则自2026年10月5日起实施，并于10月7日公布。</p>

<h3><strong>一、最新收入评分标</strong><strong>准</strong></h3>

<p>根据新的评分规则，申请人在过去五年内，如果有任意一个年度的加拿大收入达到80,000加元，即可在Canadian Work Experience: Earnings History评分项目中获得最高10分。</p>

<div style="overflow-x:auto">
<table class="oinp-earnings-table">
<tr><th scope="col">加拿大年度收入</th><th scope="col">EOI分数</th></tr>
<tr><td>$80,000或以上</td><td>10分</td></tr>
<tr><td>$70,000–$79,999</td><td>8分</td></tr>
<tr><td>$50,000–$69,999</td><td>6分</td></tr>
<tr><td>$30,000–$49,999</td><td>4分</td></tr>
<tr><td>低于$30,000</td><td>0分</td></tr>
</table>
</div>

<p>此前，年收入达到70,000加元即可获得该项目最高8分。此次调整新增了80,000加元的最高评分档次。</p>

<h3><strong>二、不是目前年薪达到</strong><strong>8</strong><strong>万就能获得</strong><strong>10</strong><strong>分</strong></h3>

<p>申请人需要特别注意，这项评分依据的是<strong>过去五年内的加拿大实际年度收入记录</strong>，而不是目前工作聘书（Job Offer）上的年薪。</p>

<p>申请人必须提供加拿大税务局（CRA）签发的Notice of Assessment（NOA）作为证明。</p>

<p>此外，收入必须来自单个年度，不能将不同年度的收入累加计算。</p>

<p>例如，一名申请人在2024年的加拿大年度收入达到85,000加元，即使目前的年薪低于80,000加元，仍可能符合新的最高收入评分标准。</p>

<h3><strong>三、对安省移民申请人的影</strong><strong>响</strong></h3>

<p>此次调整有利于过去五年曾在加拿大获得较高收入的申请人。</p>

<p>根据公布的信息，OINP已自动调整受影响的现有EOI档案分数，申请人通常无须重新注册。</p>

<p>不过，<strong>获得更高</strong><strong>EOI</strong><strong>分数并不代表一定能够获得省提名邀请</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<p>申请人仍需符合Ontario Workforce Priority Stream的其他资格要求，并在省政府的邀请筛选中具备竞争力。</p>

<h3><strong>四、申请提</strong><strong>醒</strong></h3>

<p>对于正在考虑通过安省省提名申请加拿大永久居民身份的人士，建议重新检查过去五年的CRA税务记录，确认是否符合新的收入评分标准。</p>

<p>特别是目前年薪不高、但过去曾有较高加拿大收入的申请人，不应忽略这项可能的评分优势。</p>

<p><strong>省提名申请不仅需要满足基本资格，更需要了解最新评分标准，合理规划申请策略</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<h2>Ontario OINP Updates EOI Scoring: Higher Earnings Can Now Earn Up to 10 Points</h2>

<p><strong>October 11, </strong><strong>2026</strong><strong> | Immigration Policy Insights</strong></p>

<p>Ontario has recently updated the Expression of Interest (EOI) scoring system under the Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP), increasing the points available to applicants with higher Canadian earnings.</p>

<p>The new scoring rules took effect on October 5, 2026, and were announced on October 7.</p>

<h3><strong>1. New Earnings History Scoring</strong></h3>

<p>Under the Ontario Workforce Priority Stream, applicants who earned at least <strong>CAD $80,000 in a single year within the past five years</strong> may now receive the maximum 10 points under the Canadian Work Experience: Earnings History factor.</p>

<p>The updated scoring system is as follows:</p>

<div style="overflow-x:auto">
<table class="oinp-earnings-table">
<tr><th scope="col">Annual Canadian Earnings</th><th scope="col">EOI Points</th></tr>
<tr><td>$80,000 or more</td><td>10</td></tr>
<tr><td>$70,000–$79,999</td><td>8</td></tr>
<tr><td>$50,000–$69,999</td><td>6</td></tr>
<tr><td>$30,000–$49,999</td><td>4</td></tr>
<tr><td>Below $30,000</td><td>0</td></tr>
</table>
</div>

<p>Previously, the maximum score under this factor was 8 points.</p>

<h3><strong>2. Current Salary Is Not the Determining Factor</strong></h3>

<p>Applicants should understand that these points are based on <strong>historical Canadian earnings, not their current salary or the salary stated in a job offer.</strong></p>

<p>Applicants must provide a Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Notice of Assessment (NOA) demonstrating qualifying earnings within the past five years.</p>

<p>Earnings from different years cannot be combined to meet the threshold.</p>

<p>For example, an applicant who earned $85,000 in Canada in 2024 may qualify for the maximum 10 points, even if their current annual salary is below $80,000.</p>

<h3><strong>3. What Does This Mean for OINP Applicants?</strong></h3>

<p>This change may benefit applicants who have previously earned higher incomes while working in Canada.</p>

<p>According to the announced changes, affected existing EOI profiles have been automatically updated, meaning applicants generally do not need to register again.</p>

<p>However, <strong>a higher EOI score does not guarantee an invitation to apply for provincial nomination.</strong></p>

<p>Applicants must still meet all other stream eligibility requirements and remain competitive under Ontario&#x27;s invitation selection process.</p>

<h3><strong>4. Important Reminder for Applicants</strong></h3>

<p>If you are considering permanent residence through the OINP, it may be worthwhile to review your CRA Notices of Assessment from the past five years.</p>

<p>Even if your current salary is relatively modest, your previous Canadian earnings may provide a scoring advantage.</p>
