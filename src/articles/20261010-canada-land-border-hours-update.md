# 11月1日起，加拿大26个陆路口岸调整开放时间：BC居民出行前请确认

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<img class="hero-image" src="/images/20261010-canada-land-border-checkpoint.png" alt="Canadian land border checkpoint with inspection booths, vehicle lanes and a Canadian flag" width="1672" height="941">

<p>计划在11月以后开车往返加拿大与美国的朋友，需要留意口岸开放时间的新安排。</p>

<p>加拿大边境服务局（CBSA）于10月9日发布公告，将调整BC省、Alberta省及Manitoba省共26个陆路口岸的服务时间。其中，BC省涉及9个口岸，Alberta省涉及4个，Manitoba省涉及13个。详情见官方的Alberta省公告及Manitoba省公告。</p>

<p>此次调整主要是为了配合三省采用全年夏令时间，并与仍然季节性调钟的美国相邻口岸保持服务时间协调。对于旅客来说，最直接的影响是：今后安排过境行程，需要重新确认所选口岸的开放时段。</p>

<p><strong>BC</strong><strong>省的新时间将于</strong><strong>2026</strong><strong>年</strong><strong>11</strong><strong>月</strong><strong>1</strong><strong>日凌晨</strong><strong>2</strong><strong>时起生效，持续至</strong><strong>2027</strong><strong>年</strong><strong>3</strong><strong>月</strong><strong>14</strong><strong>日凌晨</strong><strong>2</strong><strong>时，均以太平洋时间为准</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<p>以上为旅客服务时间，部分口岸的商业货运服务另有时段及节假日安排。具体以CBSA的BC省官方公告为准。</p>

<p><strong>这项公告涉及边境运营时间</strong><strong>。</strong>对准备陆路入境的客户而言，需要调整的首先是行程安排：预计何时到达、口岸届时是否开放，以及是否需要准备其他过境路线。</p>

<p>从加拿大持牌移民顾问（RCIC）的实务角度，还应提醒客户：<strong>口岸开放，并不意味着所有移民手续都可以在该时段办理。</strong> 如行程涉及特定移民手续，应提前确认该口岸实际提供的服务，并核对适用于个人情况的办理要求，不能仅凭开放时间决定前往。</p>

<p>建议旅客出发前确认加拿大与美国两侧口岸的服务时间，注意当地时区，并为路况和排队预留余量。尤其是计划晚间过境的旅客，不宜把到达时间安排在口岸关闭前的最后几分钟。</p>

<p>对已经安排11月及之后陆路行程的朋友，现在就可以检查路线和预计到达时间。提前确认这些细节，有助于减少临时改道，也能避免因口岸已经关闭而耽误行程。</p>

<h2>Canada to Adjust Hours at 26 Land Border Crossings on November 1: What BC Travellers Should Know</h2>
<p>If you plan to drive between Canada and the United States from November onward, check the updated operating hours before travelling.</p>

<p>On October 9, the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) announced changes to service hours at 26 land ports of entry across British Columbia, Alberta and Manitoba. The changes affect nine crossings in BC, four in Alberta and 13 in Manitoba. Details are available in the official announcements for <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/news/2026/10/the-cbsa-is-adjusting-hours-of-service-at-four-land-ports-of-entry-in-alberta.html">Alberta</a> and <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/news/2026/10/the-cbsa-is-adjusting-hours-of-service-at-13-land-ports-of-entry-in-manitoba.html">Manitoba</a>.</p>

<p>The adjustments follow the three provinces’ adoption of permanent year-round daylight saving time. Since neighbouring US jurisdictions will continue changing their clocks seasonally, the revised hours will keep services coordinated on both sides of the border.</p>

<p><strong>In BC, the new hours take effect at 2:00 a.m. on November 1, 2026, and remain in place until 2:00 a.m. on March 14, 2027. All times below are Pacific Time.</strong></p>

<p>Commercial service hours differ at some crossings, including weekday and holiday restrictions. Refer to the <a href="https://www.canada.ca/en/border-services-agency/news/2026/10/the-cbsa-is-adjusting-hours-of-service-at-nine-land-ports-of-entry-in-british-columbia.html">official CBSA announcement for BC</a> for the full schedule.</p>

<p><strong>This announcement concerns border operating hours.</strong> Its immediate impact on travellers is practical: when to arrive, whether the selected crossing will be open, and whether an alternative route is needed.</p>

<p>From a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant (RCIC) perspective, another distinction matters: <strong>an open border crossing does not mean every immigration procedure is available during those hours.</strong> Clients travelling for a specific immigration purpose should confirm the services offered at their chosen port of entry and the requirements applicable to their circumstances before making travel arrangements.</p>

<p>Before departure, check operating hours on both the Canadian and US sides, pay attention to local time zones, and allow extra time for traffic and border queues. Travellers planning an evening crossing should avoid scheduling their arrival just before closing.</p>

<p>If you already have a land-border trip planned for November or later, review your route and expected arrival time now. A little preparation can help prevent an unexpected detour or a delay caused by arriving after the crossing has closed.</p>
