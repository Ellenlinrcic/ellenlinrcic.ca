# 申请学签是不是资金越多越好？

<p><strong>Study Permit Proof of Funds: More Is Not Always Better</strong></p>

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

申请加拿大学签，有些人希望提供多一些存款和投资证明，让申请看起来更有保障。但主动提交作为经济能力证明的资金，都可能需要解释来源。

近期一宗联邦法院判例就涉及这个问题：即使其中一个账户的余额可能已经足够，申请人既然同时提供了其他投资来证明经济能力，签证官就可以审查这些投资是如何积累的。

因此，准备材料时，不能只看“有多少钱”，还要看这些钱属于谁、从哪里来、是否与收入情况相符，以及能否能真实用于留学。

例如，大额资金转入需要解释转账来源；配偶或父母提供资助，需要有相应的收入和资金积累证据；定期存款及投资，则需要说明其用途。

因此，资金很多却缺少解释，反而会增加签证官对申请的疑问。

资金证明的重点，是足够、可用、来源清楚。按要求如实披露，把用于学习的资金解释完整，比单纯提供大额存单更有说服力。

<h2>Study Permit Proof of Funds: More Is Not Always Better</h2>
<p>When applying for a Canadian study permit, some applicants submit additional bank statements and investment records hoping to strengthen their application. However, any funds presented as evidence of financial capacity may require an explanation of their source.</p>
<p>A recent Federal Court decision illustrates this point: even if one account may contain enough money to cover the proposed studies, an officer may still examine the source of other investments relied on in the application.</p>
<p>Preparing financial evidence therefore involves more than showing a balance. It should explain who owns the funds, how they were accumulated, whether they are consistent with the stated income, and whether they are available for study expenses.</p>
<p>For example, large deposits may need supporting transfer records. Financial support from a spouse or parent should be supported by evidence of their income and savings. Term deposits and investments may also require an explanation of when and how the funds can be accessed.</p>
<p>When reviewing study permit financial documents, we look at whether account balances, income records and fund transfers tell a consistent financial story. A substantial balance with little supporting explanation can create additional questions.</p>
<p>The goal is to demonstrate sufficient, accessible funds with a clear source. Provide all required disclosures truthfully and support the funds you rely on, rather than simply adding more assets.</p>
