# 安省省提名调整评分标准：年收入达到8万加元可获最高10分

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<img class="hero-image" style="height:auto" src="/images/oinp-earnings-history-scoring-2026.jpg" alt="Toronto skyline, Canadian flag, calculator and generic tax documents illustrating OINP earnings history scoring" width="1672" height="941">

安大略省省提名计划（OINP）近日调整了Ontario Workforce Priority Stream的意向表达（Expression of Interest，简称EOI）评分标准，提高了高收入申请人在加拿大工作收入记录方面的评分。

新规则自2026年10月5日起实施，并于10月7日公布。

## 一、最新收入评分标准

根据新的评分规则，申请人在过去五年内，如果有任意一个年度的加拿大收入达到80,000加元，即可在Canadian Work Experience: Earnings History评分项目中获得最高10分。

此前，年收入达到70,000加元即可获得该项目最高8分。此次调整新增了80,000加元的最高评分档次。

## 二、不是目前年薪达到8万就能获得10分

申请人需要特别注意，这项评分依据的是过去五年内的加拿大实际年度收入记录，而不是目前工作聘书（Job Offer）上的年薪。

申请人必须提供加拿大税务局（CRA）签发的Notice of Assessment（NOA）作为证明。

此外，收入必须来自单个年度，不能将不同年度的收入累加计算。

例如，一名申请人在2024年的加拿大年度收入达到85,000加元，即使目前的年薪低于80,000加元，仍可能符合新的最高收入评分标准。

## 三、对安省移民申请人的影响

此次调整有利于过去五年曾在加拿大获得较高收入的申请人。

根据公布的信息，OINP已自动调整受影响的现有EOI档案分数，申请人通常无须重新注册。

不过，获得更高EOI分数并不代表一定能够获得省提名邀请。

申请人仍需符合Ontario Workforce Priority Stream的其他资格要求，并在省政府的邀请筛选中具备竞争力。

## 四、申请提醒

对于正在考虑通过安省省提名申请加拿大永久居民身份的人士，建议重新检查过去五年的CRA税务记录，确认是否符合新的收入评分标准。

特别是目前年薪不高、但过去曾有较高加拿大收入的申请人，不应忽略这项可能的评分优势。

省提名申请不仅需要满足基本资格，更需要了解最新评分标准，合理规划申请策略。

## Ontario OINP Updates EOI Scoring: Higher Earnings Can Now Earn Up to 10 Points

Ontario has recently updated the Expression of Interest (EOI) scoring system under the Ontario Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP), increasing the points available to applicants with higher Canadian earnings.

The new scoring rules took effect on October 5, 2026, and were announced on October 7.

### 1. New Earnings History Scoring

Under the Ontario Workforce Priority Stream, applicants who earned at least CAD $80,000 in a single year within the past five years may now receive the maximum 10 points under the Canadian Work Experience: Earnings History factor.

The updated scoring system is as follows:

Previously, the maximum score under this factor was 8 points.

### 2. Current Salary Is Not the Determining Factor

Applicants should understand that these points are based on historical Canadian earnings, not their current salary or the salary stated in a job offer.

Applicants must provide a Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) Notice of Assessment (NOA) demonstrating qualifying earnings within the past five years.

Earnings from different years cannot be combined to meet the threshold.

For example, an applicant who earned $85,000 in Canada in 2024 may qualify for the maximum 10 points, even if their current annual salary is below $80,000.

### 3. What Does This Mean for OINP Applicants?

This change may benefit applicants who have previously earned higher incomes while working in Canada.

According to the announced changes, affected existing EOI profiles have been automatically updated, meaning applicants generally do not need to register again.

However, a higher EOI score does not guarantee an invitation to apply for provincial nomination.

Applicants must still meet all other stream eligibility requirements and remain competitive under Ontario's invitation selection process.

### 4. Important Reminder for Applicants

If you are considering permanent residence through the OINP, it may be worthwhile to review your CRA Notices of Assessment from the past five years.

Even if your current salary is relatively modest, your previous Canadian earnings may provide a scoring advantage.

Successful immigration planning requires more than meeting minimum eligibility requirements. Understanding the latest selection criteria can make a meaningful difference.
