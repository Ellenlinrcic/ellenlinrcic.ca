# 加拿大H&C人道主义申请：IRCC更新内部审理指引

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<img class="hero-image" src="../images/20261006-ircc-humanitarian-compassionate-guidelines.png" alt="法院立柱与木质法槌，人道主义及同情理由申请文章主题配图">

<p>2026年10月6日，加拿大移民局（IRCC）更新了人道主义及同情理由申请（Humanitarian and Compassionate Considerations，简称H&amp;C）的内部审理指引，进一步说明多次递交H&amp;C申请，以及H&amp;C与其他永久居民申请同时存在时的处理方式。</p>

<p>此次更新属于程序性调整，并不意味着H&amp;C申请条件全面放宽。</p>

<h2>一、H&amp;C申请被拒后，还可以重新申请吗？</h2>

<p>可以。</p>

<p>IRCC明确指出，如果申请人曾经接受H&amp;C评估，之后再次提出申请，移民官可以参考之前申请中的资料及审理结论。</p>

<p>但与此同时，移民官也必须考虑新申请中提交的新证据和新情况。</p>

<p>因此，重新递交H&amp;C申请时，重点不应只是重复之前的理由，而应充分说明申请人的情况发生了哪些变化，以及新增证据如何支持人道主义考虑。</p>

<h2>二、H&amp;C与配偶担保申请可以同时进行吗？</h2>

<p>在特定情况下，申请人可能同时有独立的H&amp;C申请和加拿大境内配偶或同居伴侣担保申请。</p>

<p>根据IRCC的处理指引：</p>

<p>如果境内配偶担保申请仍在审理，独立的H&amp;C申请通常会被暂缓处理。</p>

<p>如果配偶担保申请最终被拒，IRCC可以恢复处理尚未完成的H&amp;C申请。</p>

<p>如果申请人已经获得永久居民身份，独立的H&amp;C申请则会按相关程序结束。</p>

<p>需要注意，独立递交H&amp;C申请，与在境内配偶担保申请中提出H&amp;C豁免请求，并不是同一种程序。 两者应分别评估。</p>

<h2>三、H&amp;C不是普通移民项目的替代途径</h2>

<p>IRCC再次强调，H&amp;C属于特殊的酌情决定机制，目的是让某些无法通过常规移民类别取得永久居民身份、但具有充分人道主义理由的申请人，有机会获得特殊考虑。</p>

<p>H&amp;C并不是一条可以自由选择的普通移民途径。</p>

<p>申请人的个人情况、在加拿大的生活联系、家庭关系、子女最佳利益及可能面临的困难，都需要结合具体事实和证据进行评估。</p>

<h2>四、申请策略比重复递交更重要</h2>

<p>对于已经遭遇拒签、家庭团聚困难，或者因特殊情况无法满足常规移民要求的申请人，H&amp;C可能是一项值得评估的法律途径。</p>

<p>但每个案件的法律问题和证据要求都不相同。</p>

<p>特别是在涉及配偶担保、家庭成员排除条款或此前申请记录的情况下，申请人更应谨慎评估不同申请途径之间的关系，避免因申请策略不当增加风险。</p>

<p>如果您正在考虑H&amp;C申请，或希望重新评估此前被拒的移民申请，建议先进行专业评估，再决定下一步申请方案。</p>

<h2>IRCC Updates Humanitarian and Compassionate (H&amp;C) Application Guidelines</h2>

<p>On October 6, 2026, Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) updated its operational guidelines for Humanitarian and Compassionate (H&amp;C) applications, providing further clarification on how consecutive and concurrent applications are processed.</p>

<p>These updates are primarily procedural and do not represent a broad relaxation of H&amp;C eligibility requirements.</p>

<h3>1. Can You Reapply for H&amp;C After a Refusal?</h3>

<p>Yes. Applicants who have previously been refused H&amp;C consideration may submit a new application.</p>

<p>IRCC officers may refer to evidence and findings from previous applications. However, they must also consider new evidence and circumstances presented in subsequent applications.</p>

<p>A successful reapplication requires more than simply repeating previous arguments. Applicants should clearly explain any changes in their circumstances and provide supporting evidence addressing the concerns raised in earlier decisions.</p>

<h3>2. Can H&amp;C and Spousal Sponsorship Applications Be Submitted at the Same Time?</h3>

<p>In certain circumstances, applicants may have both a standalone H&amp;C application and an inland spousal or common-law partner sponsorship application.</p>

<p>Under IRCC&#x27;s processing guidelines:</p>

<p>A standalone H&amp;C application may be placed on hold while an inland spousal sponsorship application is being processed.</p>

<p>If the sponsorship application is refused, processing of the pending H&amp;C application may resume.</p>

<p>If the applicant obtains permanent residence, the separate H&amp;C application will be closed in accordance with applicable procedures.</p>

<p>Importantly, a standalone H&amp;C application is different from requesting H&amp;C relief within an existing spousal sponsorship application. Each involves distinct legal and procedural considerations.</p>

<h3>3. H&amp;C Is Not an Alternative to Regular Immigration Programs</h3>

<p>H&amp;C consideration is an exceptional discretionary mechanism intended for individuals whose circumstances may justify an exemption from certain immigration requirements.</p>

<p>Officers assess each application based on its individual merits, including factors such as establishment in Canada, family relationships, the best interests of affected children, and hardship.</p>

<p>A compelling application requires credible evidence demonstrating why humanitarian and compassionate relief is justified.</p>

<h3>4. Why Application Strategy Matters</h3>

<p>For individuals facing immigration refusals, family sponsorship complications, or difficulties meeting regular immigration requirements, H&amp;C consideration may provide a possible pathway to permanent residence.</p>

<p>However, submitting multiple applications without a clear legal strategy may create additional complications.</p>

<p>Cases involving spousal sponsorship, excluded family members, or previous immigration declarations require particularly careful assessment.</p>

<p>Understanding the relationship between different immigration applications and presenting consistent, well-supported evidence are essential to developing an effective strategy.</p>
