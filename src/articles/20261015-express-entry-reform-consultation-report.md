# Express Entry改革咨询报告公布：申请人应关注什么？

<p><strong>Express Entry Reform Consultation Report: What Should Applicants Watch?</strong></p>

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<p style="font-size:13px; color:#5f6b73;">政策主题示意配图｜咨询建议不等于现行规定。</p>

<p>IRCC于10月15日公布Express Entry改革咨询最终报告，汇总各方对项目整合及综合排名系统（CRS）调整的意见。<strong>这份报告本身没有使拟议改革生效</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<h2><strong>哪些方向值得关注</strong><strong>？</strong></h2>

<p>讨论内容包括合并联邦技术移民（FSW）、加拿大经验类（CEC）及联邦技工移民（FST），并调整最低语言要求；同时考虑高工资职业相关评分，检视年龄、加拿大教育、配偶、兄弟姐妹及职业资格认证的计分方式。以上仍属于改革讨论方向。</p>

<h2><strong>咨询意见不等于最终决</strong><strong>定</strong></h2>

<p>调查中，41%的受访者支持合并三个项目，46%倾向保留独立项目。这些结果反映参与者意见，不代表加拿大整体民意，也不代表IRCC最终决定。报告指出，后续可能涉及法规及部长指令调整；拟议法规修订将通过《加拿大公报》第一部分公布。</p>

<h2><strong>申请提</strong><strong>醒</strong></h2>

<p><strong>不要仅凭咨询报告修改EE档案，或假设自己已经获得新的加分。</strong>档案仍应准确反映实际情况，并依据适用规则更新。移民规划可以关注改革方向，但具体行动应以正式规则、生效日期及过渡安排为依据。</p>

<h2><strong>Express Entry Reform Consultation Report: What Should Applicants Watch?</strong></h2>

<p>IRCC published its final Express Entry reform consultation report on October 15, summarizing feedback on program consolidation and changes to the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). <strong>The report itself does not bring the proposed reforms into force.</strong></p>

<h3><strong>What Is Under Consideration?</strong></h3>

<p>Topics include merging the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Canadian Experience Class and Federal Skilled Trades Program, alongside changes to minimum language requirements. Other proposals concern high-wage occupations and scoring for age, Canadian education, spouses, siblings and occupational qualifications. These remain reform proposals.</p>

<h3><strong>Feedback Is Not a Final Decision</strong></h3>

<p>Among survey respondents, 41% supported merging the programs and 46% preferred keeping them separate. These findings represent participants’ views, not the Canadian population or IRCC’s final decision.</p>

<p>Further changes may require regulatory amendments and revised Ministerial Instructions. Proposed regulatory amendments will be published in the Canada Gazette, Part I.</p>

<h3><strong>What Should Applicants Do?</strong></h3>

<p><strong>Do not change your Express Entry profile or claim new points solely because of this report.</strong> Keep your information accurate and follow applicable rules. Planning decisions should reflect formally adopted requirements, effective dates and transition provisions.</p>
