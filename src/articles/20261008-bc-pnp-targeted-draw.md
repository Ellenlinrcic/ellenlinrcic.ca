# BC省提名10月8日定向抽选：幼教、医疗、兽医及建筑技工获邀

卑诗省提名计划（BC PNP）于2026年10月8日举行Skills Immigration职业定向抽选，向幼教、医疗、兽医护理及建筑技工领域的符合条件申请人发出申请邀请。

本轮幼教、医疗及建筑技工三个类别合计发出283份邀请，另有少于5份兽医护理类别邀请。由于官方没有披露兽医类别的确切人数，本轮总邀请数不能确定为287份。

## 本轮邀请针对哪些申请人？

幼教类别仅针对幼儿教育工作者（Early Childhood Educators），不能将其理解为所有托育相关岗位均在本轮邀请范围内。

医疗类别涵盖BC省优先医疗职业清单中的职业；建筑技工类别则涵盖省内优先建筑职业清单中的职业。

兽医护理类别的筛选范围包括优先兽医护理职业，以及持有有效专业资格的动物卫生技术员和兽医技术员（NOC 32104）。

申请人除了核对职业是否在筛选范围内，还需要确认自己符合相关专业资格及项目要求。

## 与上一轮相比，分数有什么变化？

与9月10日同类定向抽选相比，幼教、医疗及建筑技工类别的最低分数均保持不变，分别为99分、76分及94分。

兽医护理类别的最低分数则由90分降至86分。这一变化仅涉及兽医护理类别，不能据此判断BC PNP整体降低了邀请门槛。

## 申请人接下来应关注什么？

已经进入BC PNP注册池的申请人，可以登录BC PNP Online账户，查看是否收到申请邀请。

尚未获邀的申请人，应结合职业范围、专业资格及项目要求，检查注册信息是否准确。达到本轮公布的最低分数，并不代表下一轮一定会收到邀请；未来的筛选条件及分数仍可能调整。

收到申请邀请意味着可以进入省提名申请阶段，并不等于已经获得省提名或永久居民身份。

<h2>BC PNP Invites Childcare, Health Care, Veterinary and Construction Workers in October 8 Draw</h2>

<p>British Columbia held targeted Skills Immigration draws on October 8, 2026, inviting candidates in selected care and construction occupations to apply for provincial nomination.</p>

<p>The province reported 283 invitations across childcare, health care and construction trades, plus fewer than five invitations in veterinary care. The exact overall total was not disclosed.</p>

<h3>Who Was Targeted?</h3>

<p>The childcare draw was limited to early childhood educators. The health care and construction draws covered occupations on the province’s respective priority lists.</p>

<p>The veterinary draw covered priority veterinary care occupations and animal health technologists and veterinary technicians under NOC 32104 with a valid professional designation.</p>

<h3>How Did Scores Change?</h3>

<p>Compared with the September 10 draws in the same categories, minimum scores remained unchanged for childcare, health care and construction trades. The veterinary care threshold fell from 90 to 86.</p>

<p>That decrease applies specifically to veterinary care and should not be interpreted as a broader reduction in BC PNP selection thresholds.</p>

<h3>What Should Candidates Do Next?</h3>

<p>Candidates already in the registration pool should log into their BC PNP Online profiles to check for an invitation. Those still waiting should review their occupation, qualifications and registration information against the relevant selection requirements.</p>

<p>Meeting the minimum score published for this draw does not guarantee an invitation in a future round. Candidates must also meet the applicable program and occupational requirements.</p>
