# 阿省提名配额进一步收紧：截至10月7日，年度剩余名额降至1,062个

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<img class="hero-image" src="/images/aaip.jpg" alt="Alberta Advantage Immigration Program AAIP">


<p>Alberta省提名计划（AAIP）最新公布的抽选及配额数据显示，截至2026年10月7日，全年6,603个提名名额中，已发出5,541份提名，剩余 1,062个，待处理申请为948份。</p>

<p>与此前报告的5,221份已发提名相比，新增了320份提名，年度剩余名额由1,382个降至1,062个。对于正在规划Alberta省提名路径的申请人，这意味着评估申请机会时，需要使用最新配额数据，并关注所属类别的名额情况。</p>

<p>其中，Priority Sector Draws and Other Initiatives仅剩3个名额，Tourism and Hospitality仅剩15个名额，值得相关申请人及顾问重点关注。</p>

<p>这些数字是官方公布的类别剩余额度，并不等于尚可提交的新申请数量。 AAIP保留重新分配名额的权利，因此，不能仅凭当前余量判断某个类别已经关闭，或未来一定不会再有邀请。</p>

<p>同样，抽选邀请与提名属于不同阶段。收到邀请并不等于已经获得提名，不能将邀请人数直接从剩余提名配额中扣除，也不能将待处理申请与剩余名额简单相减，推算新申请容量。</p>

<p>对于准备进入Alberta Priority Sector相关路径的客户，应及时重新评估申请时间安排及提名机会，避免继续沿用9月的配额数字。</p>

<p>评估时，需要区分客户目前所处阶段：准备提交Worker EOI、已进入候选池、已收到邀请，或已递交申请等待审核。不同阶段受到的影响并不相同。</p>

<p>近期最低EOI分数可以作为参考，但申请规划仍需结合目标类别的剩余额度及后续官方更新。随着年度名额减少，申请人及顾问应及时调整时间安排和预期，并评估其他可行路径。</p>

<p>官方抽选、处理进度及配额信息：<a href="https://www.alberta.ca/aaip-processing-information">AAIP Processing Information</a>。</p>

<h2>Alberta’s AAIP Allocation Tightens: Only 1,062 Nomination Spaces Remain as of October 7</h2>

<p>As of October 7, 2026, the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) had issued 5,541 nominations against its annual allocation of 6,603, leaving 1,062 nomination spaces. The program also reported 948 applications in process.</p>

<p>Compared with the previously reported total of 5,221 nominations issued, this represents an increase of 320 nominations. The remaining annual allocation has consequently declined from 1,382 to 1,062 spaces. Applicants considering an Alberta nomination should use the updated figures when assessing their timelines and the availability of spaces in their intended category.</p>

<p>The three remaining spaces under Priority Sector Draws and Other Initiatives, and the 15 remaining under Tourism and Hospitality, deserve particular attention from applicants and their representatives.</p>

<p>These figures reflect the published remaining nomination allocations; they do not indicate how many new applications can still be submitted. The AAIP reserves the right to reallocate spaces. The current figures alone therefore do not establish that a category is closed or that no further invitations will be issued.</p>

<p>Invitations and nominations also represent different stages of the process. Receiving an invitation does not mean an applicant has received a nomination. Invitation totals should not be directly deducted from the remaining nomination allocation, and applications in process should not simply be subtracted from remaining spaces to estimate capacity for new applications.</p>

<p>For clients considering Alberta’s Priority Sector pathways, application timelines and nomination prospects should be reassessed using the October 7 figures. Planning should no longer rely on September allocation data.</p>

<p>The assessment should account for each client’s current stage: preparing a Worker EOI, already in the candidate pool, holding an invitation, or awaiting a decision on a submitted application. The implications differ across these stages.</p>

<p>Recent minimum EOI scores offer historical context, but planning should also consider the remaining allocation in the relevant category and subsequent official updates. As the annual allocation becomes more limited, applicants and Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultants (RCICs) should review timelines, manage expectations, and assess other available pathways.</p>
