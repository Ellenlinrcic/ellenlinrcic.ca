# PEI省提名最新抽选：10月7日发出90份邀请，继续优先紧缺行业

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<img class="hero-image" src="../images/PEIPNP_Draw_Reusable.jpg" alt="Prince Edward Island PEI Provincial Nominee Program">

<p>2026年10月7日，爱德华王子岛省（PEI）进行了新一轮省提名EOI抽选，共发出90份邀请。</p>

<p>本轮邀请全部属于Labour &amp; Express Entry类别，Business Work Permit Entrepreneur企业家类别没有发出邀请。</p>

<h2>2026年10月7日抽选结果</h2>

<p>Labour &amp; Express Entry： 90份邀请</p>

<p>Business Work Permit Entrepreneur： 0份</p>

<p>最低邀请分数： 未公布</p>

<p>2026年累计邀请： 1,319份</p>

<p>上一轮抽选于9月17日举行，共发出195份邀请。</p>

<h2>PEI目前优先考虑哪些申请人？</h2>

<p>PEI继续重点关注医疗、技工、制造业及其他劳动力短缺行业。</p>

<p>相比之下，销售及服务行业的申请人可能面临较低的邀请机会。</p>

<p>需要注意的是，本轮抽选没有公布具体职业代码（NOC）或最低分数，因此不能认为全部90份邀请都来自上述优先行业。</p>

<h2>对申请人的提醒</h2>

<p>符合省提名申请条件，并不意味着一定能够获得邀请。</p>

<p>在目前的筛选机制下，申请人的职业、雇主支持、工作经验及是否符合PEI劳动力需求，都可能影响获得邀请的机会。</p>

<p>对于计划通过PEI省提名取得加拿大永久居民身份的申请人，建议提前联系我们评估职业及申请条件，制定适合自己的移民方案。</p>

<h2>PEI PNP Update: 90 Invitations Issued on October 7, 2026</h2>

<p>On October 7, 2026, Prince Edward Island (PEI) conducted a new Expression of Interest (EOI) draw under its Provincial Nominee Program (PEI PNP), issuing 90 Invitations to Apply (ITAs).</p>

<p>All invitations were issued under the Labour &amp; Express Entry category. No invitations were issued under the Business Work Permit Entrepreneur Stream.</p>

<h3>October 7, 2026 Draw Results</h3>

<p>Labour &amp; Express Entry: 90 invitations</p>

<p>Business Work Permit Entrepreneur: 0 invitations</p>

<p>Minimum EOI Score: Not disclosed</p>

<p>Total Invitations in 2026: 1,319</p>

<p>The previous draw took place on September 17, 2026, when 195 invitations were issued.</p>

<h3>Which Applicants Are Being Prioritized?</h3>

<p>PEI continues to prioritize applicants working in sectors experiencing labour shortages, particularly healthcare, skilled trades, manufacturing, and other high-demand occupations.</p>

<p>Applicants working in sales and service occupations may face more limited opportunities to receive invitations.</p>

<p>However, PEI has not published specific National Occupational Classification (NOC) codes or minimum scores for this draw.</p>

<h3>What Does This Mean for Applicants?</h3>

<p>Meeting the eligibility requirements for PEI PNP does not guarantee an invitation.</p>

<p>Under the current selection system, factors such as occupation, employer support, work experience, and alignment with PEI&#x27;s labour market priorities may influence an applicant&#x27;s chances of receiving an invitation.</p>

<p>Applicants considering PEI PNP should carefully assess their eligibility and develop an immigration strategy based on current provincial priorities.</p>
