# 加拿大难民申请网上流程将于11月3日调整：未提交申请请留意

<p><strong>Canada’s Online Asylum Claim Process Changes November 3: A Reminder for Unsubmitted Claims</strong></p>

<a class="language-jump" href="#english-version">Read in English</a>

<p>加拿大移民局（IRCC）宣布，自 <strong>2026</strong><strong>年</strong><strong>11</strong><strong>月</strong><strong>3</strong><strong>日</strong>起，IRCC Portal 内的难民保护申请提交流程将有所调整。已经开始填写、但尚未提交申请的人士，应提前检查账户及材料。</p>

<h2><strong>已开始填写的申请，会受到什么影响</strong><strong>？</strong></h2>

<p>如果在 <strong>2026</strong><strong>年</strong><strong>11</strong><strong>月</strong><strong>2</strong><strong>日晚上</strong><strong>11:59</strong><strong>（加拿大东部时间）之前</strong>完成提交，申请人无须仅因这次流程调整重新提供资料，除非IRCC另行要求。</p>

<p>如果届时仍未提交，已填写的个人资料会保留，但申请人需要按照新流程回答新增问题，并重新检查此前的答案。</p>

<p><strong>保存草稿不等于正式提交。</strong>申请人应确认账户显示的实际状态，并保留提交确认记录。</p>

<h2><strong>在入境口岸提出的申请，也要检</strong><strong>查</strong></h2>

<p>如果您已向加拿大边境服务局（CBSA）提出难民申请，并被要求到网上完成后续步骤，应检查申请是否已关联至 Portal 账户，以及是否已正式提交。</p>

<p>根据目前官方说明，未在上述时间前完成关联或提交的人士，仍须完成关联、回答新增问题并复核旧答案；<strong>CBSA</strong><strong>此前收集的资料会保留</strong><strong>。</strong></p>

<h2><strong>申请提</strong><strong>醒</strong></h2>

<p>上述日期是网上流程转换的时间节点，不应视为所有难民申请统一适用的递交期限。您仍须遵守IRCC、CBSA或加拿大移民及难民委员会（IRB）对个案发出的要求和期限。</p>

<p>建议提前核对本人及家庭成员资料、申请理由和支持文件，避免临近转换时间才发现遗漏，也不要为了赶时间提交未经核对的答案。</p>

<p>如果您正在准备难民申请，或需要协助完成CBSA要求的网上步骤，欢迎联系我们评估具体程序及文件要求。</p>

<h2><strong>Canada’s Online Asylum Claim Process Changes November 3: A Reminder for Unsubmitted Claims</strong></h2>

<p>Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) will change how asylum claims are submitted through the IRCC Portal on <strong>November 3, 2026</strong>. Applicants with unfinished claims should review their accounts and documents before the transition.</p>

<h3><strong>What Happens to an Unfinished Claim?</strong></h3>

<p>If you submit your claim by <strong>11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on November 2, 2026</strong>, you will not need to provide additional information because of this change unless IRCC requests it.</p>

<p>If your claim remains unsubmitted, your personal information will be saved, but you will need to answer new questions and review previous answers.</p>

<p><strong>Saving a draft is not the same as submitting a claim.</strong> Check your account status and keep your submission confirmation.</p>

<h3><strong>Claims Started at a Port of Entry</strong></h3>

<p>If the Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) instructed you to complete your claim online, check whether it has been linked to your Portal account and submitted.</p>

<p>Under the current official instructions, applicants who have not completed these steps before the transition must complete the linking process, answer new questions and review previous answers. <strong>Information previously collected by CBSA will be saved.</strong></p>

<h3><strong>Prepare Carefully</strong></h3>

<p>This is a Portal transition cutoff, not a universal filing deadline. Any individual deadlines imposed by IRCC, CBSA or the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada (IRB) still apply.</p>

<p>Review personal and family details, the basis of your claim and supporting documents early. Do not submit unchecked answers simply to meet the transition cutoff.</p>

<p>For assistance preparing a claim or completing the online steps requested by CBSA, contact our office for an assessment.</p>
