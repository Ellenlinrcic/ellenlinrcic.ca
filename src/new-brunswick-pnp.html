New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program

New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program（NBPNP）是新不伦瑞克省根据经济和劳动力需求，提名计划在当地生活、工作或经营企业的合资格申请人的移民项目。

主要方向包括技术工人、Express Entry、面向法语申请人的战略倡议，以及商业移民。不同类别分别关注当地就业、省内联系、语言背景或企业经营计划。

The New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program (NBPNP) nominates eligible applicants who intend to live, work or operate a business in the province. Its principal streams include Skilled Worker, Express Entry, Strategic Initiative and Business Immigration.

什么是NBPNP？ | What Is NBPNP?

NBPNP不是单一的雇主担保项目。技术工人类别与合资格就业及雇主支持相关；Express Entry类别要求申请人同时满足联邦快速通道及省级条件。

战略倡议类别主要面向法语申请人；商业移民类别则关注计划创办或购买企业、并参与日常经营管理的企业家。申请人应按自己的工作、语言和经营背景选择相应类别。

NBPNP采用EOI及邀请流程，省政府依据需求、名额和选择重点邀请候选人。符合基本资格或提交EOI不保证获邀；获得省提名后，永久居民申请仍由联邦政府审批。

NBPNP streams serve different profiles: employer-supported workers, eligible Express Entry candidates, French-speaking applicants and entrepreneurs who will actively manage a local business. Stream-specific criteria and selection requirements must be assessed separately.

An expression of interest is not a complete nomination application and does not guarantee an invitation. Provincial nomination and the federal permanent residence decision are separate stages.

NBPNP主要项目与申请规划 | Pathways & Application Planning

根据个人情况区分适用类别，并了解雇主支持、EOI及后续材料准备。

Skilled Worker

技术工人类别
Skilled Worker

For workers employed in New Brunswick or holding a qualifying full-time, non-seasonal offer. The New Brunswick Experience, Graduates and Priority Occupations pathways have distinct conditions. Review employer support and the pathway that fits your circumstances.

适用于在新不伦瑞克省工作或持有符合要求的全职、非季节性工作机会的申请人。省内经验、毕业生及优先职业方向的条件不同，需要结合工作经历、毕业背景和雇主支持逐一核对。

官方指引 | Official guidance →
Express Entry

快速通道类别
Express Entry

Candidates need a valid federal Express Entry profile as well as provincial eligibility. Employment in New Brunswick and New Brunswick Interest are distinct pathways; entering the federal pool alone is not sufficient.

申请人须同时满足联邦快速通道及省级要求。省内就业与省政府意向选择属于不同方向，应核对就业及居住记录、语言、学历和适用的选择条件。仅进入联邦候选池不等于符合省提名。

官方指引 | Official guidance →
Strategic Initiative

法语战略倡议类别
Strategic Initiative

For French-speaking candidates intending to live and work in New Brunswick. Pathways cover Francophone workers, provincial Francophone priorities and qualifying remote workers. Language results and the specific connection requirements matter.

面向计划在该省生活和工作的法语申请人，包括法语工人、法语优先方向及符合要求的远程工作者。需要分别核对法语成绩、工作、居住及省内联系，不能只凭法语能力判断资格。

官方指引 | Official guidance →
Business Immigration

商业移民类别
Business Immigration

Entrepreneurs must establish or purchase a business and participate in daily management. Review management experience, investment, net worth and the business plan. Nomination follows the required approval and business-performance stages.

适合计划在当地创办或购买企业并参与日常管理的申请人。评估包括经营管理经验、资金来源、净资产、投资和商业计划，获批及实际经营阶段与最终省提名需要分别规划。

官方指引 | Official guidance →
Expression of Interest

EOI与邀请流程
Expression of Interest

An EOI is an initial expression of interest, not a complete nomination application. Selection reflects provincial needs and available nominations. Prepare evidence supporting the information declared before responding to an invitation.

EOI是申请意向，并非完整省提名申请。省政府结合需求及名额选择候选人；符合条件仍不保证获邀。提交前应核对申报信息，获邀后按要求准备证据及完整材料。

官方指引 | Official guidance →
Employer & Settlement

雇主支持与定居规划
Employer & Settlement

Review the employment contract, duties, wage, employer documentation and your settlement plans together. A job title alone does not establish eligibility. Identify documentary gaps before choosing an application strategy.

应将合同、岗位职责、工资、雇主材料及定居计划结合评估。职位名称本身不能代替职业分类或项目资格分析；申请前应梳理证明材料，核对工作和身份记录是否一致。

官方指引 | Official guidance →

资格、选择与申请准备需要一起评估

满足某个类别的基本要求，与能够立即提交完整申请或获得提名，并不是同一件事。应先确认适用类别及其当前受理方式，再核对雇主、职位、语言和身份材料。

涉及EOI的申请，还需检查评分或选择因素及申报信息的证明材料。等待期间应关注工作许可、语言成绩和其他文件的有效期。

Assess eligibility, selection and document readiness together. Confirm the correct stream and intake process, and keep employment and status information current while preparing your application.
