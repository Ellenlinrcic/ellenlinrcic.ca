PEI省提名
Prince Edward Island Provincial Nominee Program

Prince Edward Island Provincial Nominee Program （PEI PNP）是爱德华王子岛省根据本省经济发展、 人口增长和劳动力市场需求， 选择符合条件的技术工人、 国际毕业生、Express Entry候选人 以及企业家的省提名移民项目。

对已经在PEI工作、 拥有当地雇主提供的长期工作机会， 或职业背景符合PEI当前劳动力短缺行业的申请人而言， PEI PNP可能成为取得加拿大永久居民身份的重要途径。

The Prince Edward Island Provincial Nominee Program allows PEI to nominate eligible workers, Express Entry candidates, international graduates and entrepreneurs who may contribute to the province’s economy and labour market. Eligibility and competitiveness may depend on occupation, industry, job offer, employer support, work experience, education, language ability, immigration status and current provincial priorities.

什么是PEI PNP？ | What Is the PEI PNP?

PEI PNP并不是单一的一条移民通道。 省政府会根据年度省提名名额、 PEI劳动力市场需求、申请数量、 职业和行业优先方向， 从符合条件的候选人中进行选择。

PEI的技术移民体系主要包括 Workforce类别及PEI Express Entry。 Workforce类别下又包含 Skilled Worker、Critical Worker、 International Graduate、 Intermediate Experience及 Occupations in Demand等不同通道。

多数Workforce通道与PEI雇主及当地工作机会密切相关。 因此，职位、NOC / TEER、 工作经验、语言能力、教育背景、 雇主支持以及申请人的加拿大身份， 都可能成为资格评估的重要因素。

PEI also operates an Expression of Interest system. Having an eligible EOI profile does not itself guarantee an invitation. Provincial nomination allocations, application volumes and current labour market priorities may affect who is selected.

PEI PNP主要项目 | Key PEI PNP Pathways

不同申请人的适用通道可能完全不同。 评估时需要结合申请人的职业、 PEI工作情况、雇主、学历、 工作经验、语言能力及当前省级选择重点。

Skilled Worker

PEI技术工人
Skilled Worker

The Skilled Worker pathways are designed for qualified workers with skilled employment connected to a Prince Edward Island employer. Separate considerations may apply to candidates already working in PEI and candidates outside Canada.

Skilled Worker类别主要适用于 拥有符合要求的技术工作及PEI雇主支持的申请人。 PEI境内申请人与境外技术人才 适用的具体条件及招聘要求可能不同。

Critical Worker

关键工人类别
Critical Worker

The Critical Worker stream is employer-driven and may provide a nomination pathway for eligible workers who are currently employed full-time in Prince Edward Island.

Critical Worker属于雇主驱动型项目， 主要适用于已经在PEI为当地雇主 全职工作的符合条件申请人。 工作职位、工作时间、雇主及语言等条件 都需要进一步审核。

International Graduate

PEI国际毕业生
International Graduate

The International Graduate stream may be available to eligible graduates of publicly funded PEI post-secondary institutions who have qualifying employment with a Prince Edward Island employer.

适合从PEI公立高等院校毕业， 并取得符合要求的PEI工作机会的国际毕业生。 毕业学校、工作许可、雇主和职位情况 都是评估重点。

Intermediate Experience

中等技能经验类别
Intermediate Experience

The Intermediate Experience stream is intended for certain workers whose occupational and work experience background meets PEI program requirements and current labour market needs.

Intermediate Experience类别 适用于符合特定工作经验、 职业及PEI雇主要求的申请人。 是否适用需要结合NOC / TEER、 过往工作经验和具体job offer判断。

Occupations in Demand

紧缺职业类别
Occupations in Demand

The Occupations in Demand stream may be used by PEI employers to recruit workers in occupations identified as important to provincial labour market needs.

该类别主要针对PEI劳动力市场 有实际招聘需求的部分职业。 职业范围和省政府优先方向可能调整， 申请前应确认当前适用政策。

Express Entry

PEI快速通道省提名
PEI Express Entry

Candidates with a valid federal Express Entry profile may submit an Expression of Interest for consideration by Prince Edward Island. A provincial nomination may substantially increase the candidate’s competitiveness in the federal Express Entry system.

已经符合联邦Express Entry体系要求的申请人， 可以根据PEI相关要求提交EOI， 由省政府根据当前经济和劳动力市场需要进行选择。

Business Work Permit

企业家工作许可类别
Business Work Permit Entrepreneur

PEI’s Business Work Permit Entrepreneur stream is intended for experienced business owners or senior managers who intend to establish, purchase and actively operate a business in Prince Edward Island.

适合具有企业经营或高级管理经验， 并计划在PEI创办、购买及实际经营企业的申请人。 评估通常需要结合商业经验、净资产、 投资能力、商业计划及实际经营安排。

Current Priorities

当前职业及行业优先方向
Provincial Priorities

PEI currently places greater emphasis on workers in sectors experiencing significant labour shortages, including healthcare, trades, manufacturing and other key industries.

目前PEI的抽选并非单纯按照EOI分数决定。 医疗、技工、制造业及其他劳动力短缺行业 更受省政府关注； Sales and Service行业的申请人 目前可能不会获得邀请。

PEI PNP现在不能只看EOI分数

PEI近年来越来越强调 申请人的职业和行业 是否符合本省当前劳动力市场需求。

因此，一名申请人即使符合某个PEI PNP项目的基本条件， 也并不代表一定会收到省政府邀请。

目前医疗、技工、制造业 以及其他劳动力短缺领域受到较高优先考虑； 相比之下，Sales and Service行业申请人 目前可能不会获得邀请。

PEI PNP competitiveness should therefore be assessed by looking at both program eligibility and current provincial selection priorities, including occupation, industry, job offer, employer, work experience, EOI profile and available nomination allocation.
