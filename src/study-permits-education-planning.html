学习许可与留学规划
Study Permits & Education Planning

在加拿大留学不仅涉及取得学校录取通知书， 还需要综合考虑学习许可申请、资金安排、 学习计划、当前身份、课程选择以及毕业后的工作和移民方向。 对于已经在加拿大的学生， 学签延期、身份恢复、转学、 学业中断及毕业后的身份衔接也可能影响未来的申请。

Studying in Canada involves more than obtaining a letter of acceptance. A successful immigration strategy may require careful consideration of study permit eligibility, financial preparation, study purpose, immigration status, program selection, and post-graduation plans. Students already in Canada may also need to address permit extensions, restoration of status, changes in studies, academic interruptions, and future work or immigration options.

学习许可与留学服务 | Study Permit & Education Services

学生所处的阶段不同， 需要解决的移民问题也不同。 从首次申请学习许可，到在加拿大延长身份、 身份恢复、拒签后的重新申请， 再到毕业后的工作许可和长期规划， 都需要结合个人情况进行判断。

Immigration considerations vary depending on where a student is in the study process. The appropriate strategy may involve an initial study permit, extension, restoration, reapplication after refusal, or post-graduation planning.

如何评估学习许可与留学规划？ | How We Assess Study Permit & Education Plans

学习许可申请并不是简单地提交录取通知书和资金证明。 申请人的教育背景、选择课程的合理性、 资金来源、身份历史以及未来规划， 都可能影响申请策略。

  • 学费及生活费用安排 | Tuition and living expense planning
  • 资金来源及财务证明 | Source of funds and financial evidence
  • 当前加拿大身份 | Current immigration status
  • 过往签证及移民申请历史 | Previous visa and immigration history
  • 家庭情况及与原居国的联系 | Family circumstances and home-country ties
  • 毕业后的工作及移民计划 | Post-graduation work and immigration plans
