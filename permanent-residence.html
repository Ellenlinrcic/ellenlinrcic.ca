加拿大永久居民规划 | Canadian Permanent Residence Planning

加拿大永久居民申请
Permanent Residence

加拿大永久居民申请有多种不同途径。 申请人可能通过联邦快速通道、配偶及家庭团聚、 难民或受保护人士类别、人道与同情因素， 或其他适用的永久居民类别申请加拿大永久居民身份。 适合的方案需要结合个人背景、工作经历、语言能力、 家庭情况、当前身份及过往移民历史进行综合判断。

Canadian permanent residence may be pursued through different pathways, including Express Entry, family sponsorship, refugee and protected person applications, humanitarian and compassionate considerations, and other applicable permanent residence categories. The appropriate strategy depends on the applicant’s qualifications, employment history, language ability, family circumstances, current status, and immigration history.

如何评估永久居民申请？ | How We Assess Permanent Residence Options

永久居民申请不能只看年龄、分数或是否有雇主。 一个完整的评估，需要结合申请人的整体背景、 现有身份、申请历史和长期定居目标。

常见评估因素 | Key Assessment Factors

  • 加拿大或海外工作经验 | Canadian and international work experience
  • 学历、专业及教育背景 | Education and academic background
  • 英语或法语能力 | English or French language ability
  • 职业类别及岗位职责 | Occupational classification and job duties
  • 当前身份及申请历史 | Current status and immigration history
  • 婚姻、家庭及亲属关系 | Marriage, family, and relationship history
  • 加拿大雇主及工作机会 | Canadian employer and employment opportunities
  • 申请风险、文件矛盾及潜在疑虑 | Application risks and potential inconsistencies
