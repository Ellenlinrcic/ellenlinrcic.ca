Status and Work Authorization Are Not the Same

A work permit strategy must address eligibility, timing, the conditions of work, the applicant’s temporary resident status, and any transition to permanent residence.

工签规划不仅要判断申请资格，还必须同时审查递交时间、工作条件、临时居民身份， 以及与永久居民申请之间的衔接。

从取得工作授权到保持合法身份
From Work Authorization to Status Continuity

There is no single work permit that fits every applicant. The correct pathway may depend on an LMIA, an LMIA exemption, graduation from an eligible program, a spouse or partner’s status, a permanent residence application, or a temporary public policy. Timing is equally important: applying after expiry may change both the applicant’s status and the right to work.

加拿大并不存在适用于所有人的统一工签。可行途径可能取决于 LMIA、LMIA 豁免、 合资格课程毕业、配偶或伴侣的身份、已经递交的永久居民申请，或特定临时公共政策。 递交时间同样重要：逾期申请可能同时影响申请人的身份及继续工作的权利。

服务范围 | Scope of Services

1

LMIA支持的工作许可
LMIA-Supported Work Permits

Work permit applications based on a positive LMIA, including review of the employment offer, LMIA decision, job duties, wages, qualifications, admissibility, and accompanying family options.

根据获批 LMIA 申请雇主指定工签，包括审查聘用条件、LMIA 决定、工作职责、工资、 申请人资历、入境许可问题及随行家庭成员的可行安排。

2

LMIA豁免工作许可
LMIA-Exempt Work Permits

Assessment and application support for eligible LMIA-exempt categories, including Francophone Mobility, international agreements, intra-company transfers, significant-benefit categories, and other applicable exemptions.

评估并代理适用的 LMIA 豁免工签，包括法语流动计划、国际协定、跨国公司内部调动、 重大利益类别及其他符合条件的豁免途径，并处理 Employer Portal 等相关要求。

3

毕业后工作许可（PGWP）
Post-Graduation Work Permits

PGWP eligibility and application review, including the institution and program, study history, completion documents, language and field-of-study requirements where applicable, application timing, and authorization to work while awaiting a decision.

审查毕业后工签资格及申请，包括学校与课程、学习历史、毕业文件、适用的语言及专业要求、 递交期限，以及等待申请结果期间是否具有合法工作授权。

4

配偶开放工作许可（SOWP）
Spousal Open Work Permits

Eligibility assessment for spouses or common-law partners of eligible workers, students, and certain permanent residence applicants, with attention to current occupation, program, permit-validity, relationship, and status requirements.

为符合条件的工人、学生及部分永久居民申请人的配偶或同居伴侣评估开放工签资格， 重点审查主申请人的职业、课程、许可有效期、双方关系及身份要求。

5

桥梁开放工作许可（BOWP）
Bridging Open Work Permits

BOWP assessment and application support for eligible permanent residence applicants, including review of the immigration class, application stage, acknowledgement documents, current work permit, nomination restrictions, residence, and timing requirements.

为符合条件的永久居民申请人评估并代理桥梁开放工签，审查其移民类别、申请阶段、 收件或受理文件、现有工签、省提名就业限制、居住地点及递交时间要求。

6

公共政策工作许可
Public Policy Work Permits

Assessment of open or employer-specific work permit measures created under active public policies, including the eligible population, regulatory exemptions, effective and expiry dates, application location, documentary requirements, and family-member eligibility.

评估现行公共政策所设立的开放式或雇主指定工签措施，包括适用人群、法规豁免、 生效及截止日期、申请地点、文件要求，以及家庭成员是否符合条件。

7

工签续签、身份维持与身份恢复
Extensions, Maintained Status & Restoration

Strategy and application support for work permit extensions, employer or condition changes, maintained status, proof of continued work authorization, restoration after status loss, and transitions to another temporary status. This includes determining whether the applicant may remain in Canada, whether they may continue working, and what must stop while an application is pending.

提供工签续签、变更雇主或工作条件、maintained status、继续工作授权证明、身份失效后的恢复， 以及转为其他临时身份的申请规划。重点判断申请人能否继续留在加拿大、能否继续工作， 以及申请审理期间必须停止哪些活动。

工作许可与身份期限需要分别审查
Work Authorization and Status Must Be Reviewed Separately

A pending application does not always authorize work. The application type, filing date, previous permit conditions, location, and travel history may all affect the result.

申请正在审理，并不当然代表申请人可以继续工作。申请类别、递交日期、原许可条件、 申请人所在地及旅行情况都可能影响结论。

  • Apply before the current status expires whenever possible. | 应尽可能在现有身份到期前递交合适的申请。
  • Maintained status does not automatically permit work under new conditions or for a new employer. | Maintained status 并不自动允许申请人按照新条件或为新雇主工作。
  • A person applying for restoration normally cannot work until the new work permit is issued. | 申请身份恢复的人通常必须停止工作，直至新的工作许可获批。
  • Visitor status or a visitor record does not by itself authorize employment. | 访客身份或 Visitor Record 本身不提供工作授权。
  • Public policies are time-limited and must be checked for current eligibility and deadlines. | 公共政策通常具有时效性，必须核实当前资格条件及申请截止日期。
