Alberta Immigration | 阿尔伯塔省移民

阿尔伯塔省提名
Alberta Advantage Immigration Program

Alberta Advantage Immigration Program （AAIP，阿尔伯塔省优势移民计划） 是阿尔伯塔省根据本省经济发展、 劳动力市场需求及人口增长目标， 选择符合条件的技术工人、 Express Entry候选人及企业家的省提名计划。

AAIP目前既有面向已经在阿省工作、 获得阿省雇主支持的申请人类别， 也有针对医疗、科技、旅游酒店、 农村社区及重点经济行业的选择机会。

The Alberta Advantage Immigration Program allows Alberta to nominate eligible workers, Express Entry candidates and entrepreneurs who can contribute to the province’s economy and labour market. Eligibility and competitiveness may depend on employment, occupation, industry, Alberta employer support, work experience, education, language ability, immigration status and current provincial priorities.

什么是AAIP？ | What Is the AAIP?

AAIP并不是单一的一条移民通道。 阿省目前设有多个Worker Streams 以及多个Entrepreneur Streams， 不同类别对工作地点、职位、 雇主、职业、语言、工作经验 及Express Entry资格的要求并不相同。

目前主要Worker Streams包括 Alberta Opportunity Stream、 Alberta Express Entry Stream、 Rural Renewal Stream 以及Tourism and Hospitality Stream。 此外，医疗职业申请人还可能通过 Dedicated Health Care Pathway申请。

Alberta Express Entry Stream 还会根据阿省经济及劳动力市场重点， 对科技、医疗、执法 以及其他重点行业进行选择。

AAIP uses a Worker Expression of Interest system. Meeting minimum eligibility requirements does not guarantee an invitation. Selection may also depend on EOI score, occupation, sector, Alberta employment, provincial priorities and available nomination allocation.

AAIP主要项目 | Key AAIP Pathways

不同申请人的适用方向差别很大。 除了项目基本资格以外， 还需要结合当前AAIP抽选方向、 职业、行业、雇主、 工签状态及EOI竞争力进行判断。

Alberta Opportunity Stream

阿省机会类别
Alberta Opportunity Stream

The Alberta Opportunity Stream is designed for eligible temporary foreign workers who are already living and working in Alberta and have qualifying employment with an Alberta employer.

Alberta Opportunity Stream主要适用于 已经在阿省工作并符合相关职位、 工作经验、语言、学历、 工作许可及雇主要求的申请人。

Express Entry

阿省快速通道省提名
Alberta Express Entry Stream

Alberta may invite candidates from the federal Express Entry pool whose profiles align with provincial economic, sector and occupational priorities.

已经进入联邦Express Entry池的申请人， 如果职业、工作背景 或个人情况符合阿省当前经济及行业重点， 可能收到AAIP邀请申请省提名。

Accelerated Tech Pathway

科技快速通道
Accelerated Tech Pathway

The Accelerated Tech Pathway operates within the Alberta Express Entry Stream and targets eligible technology workers whose occupation and Alberta employment meet current pathway requirements.

该通道适用于符合条件的科技行业申请人， 需要结合Express Entry资格、 职业、阿省Job Offer或就业情况 以及当前认可职业范围进行判断。

Dedicated Health Care

医疗专属通道
Dedicated Health Care Pathway

The Dedicated Health Care Pathway is available to eligible health care professionals with qualifying Alberta job offers. Both Express Entry and non-Express Entry options are available.

适用于符合条件、 获得阿省合资格医疗职位Job Offer 并满足相关执业或监管要求的医疗专业人员。 目前设有Express Entry 及Non-Express Entry两种申请方式。

Rural Renewal

阿省农村更新类别
Rural Renewal Stream

The Rural Renewal Stream supports designated rural communities in attracting and retaining newcomers to address local labour shortages.

适用于计划在阿省指定农村社区 工作和定居的申请人。 通常需要符合项目要求的当地工作机会 以及指定社区的支持。

Tourism & Hospitality

旅游与酒店业类别
Tourism and Hospitality Stream

This stream is intended for eligible workers who are already living and working in Alberta in qualifying tourism and hospitality occupations and have a full-time job offer from an eligible Alberta employer.

主要适用于已经在阿省旅游及酒店行业 符合要求岗位工作的申请人， 并需要符合职业、雇主、 工作时间及其他项目条件。

Priority Sectors

阿省重点行业选择
Priority Sector Selection

Alberta may conduct targeted selections aligned with provincial labour market priorities, including sectors such as health care, construction, agriculture, manufacturing, aviation and skilled trades.

AAIP会根据阿省劳动力市场需求， 对部分重点行业进行定向选择。 近年来包括医疗、建筑、 农业、制造、航空 及相关技工职业。

Law Enforcement

执法职业通道
Law Enforcement Pathway

Alberta also operates a dedicated law enforcement pathway within the Alberta Express Entry Stream for eligible candidates connected to qualifying police occupations.

阿省Express Entry体系内 还设有针对符合条件执法职业申请人的 Law Enforcement Pathway。 具体资格需要结合职位、 雇佣情况及当期项目要求判断。

Rural Entrepreneur

农村企业家类别
Rural Entrepreneur Stream

The Rural Entrepreneur Stream is designed for experienced entrepreneurs who plan to start a new business or purchase an existing business in an eligible rural Alberta community.

适合具有企业经营或管理经验， 并希望在符合条件的阿省农村社区 创办或购买企业的申请人。

Graduate Entrepreneur

阿省毕业生企业家
Graduate Entrepreneur Stream

This stream is intended for eligible international graduates of approved Alberta post-secondary institutions who plan to establish or purchase a business in Alberta.

主要适用于符合条件的阿省国际毕业生， 计划在阿省创办或购买企业并实际经营。

Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur

海外毕业生企业家
Foreign Graduate Entrepreneur Stream

This stream is designed for eligible foreign graduates who obtained a degree outside Canada and intend to establish an innovative start-up business in Alberta.

适用于在加拿大境外取得学历、 并计划在阿省建立创新型创业企业的申请人， 通常需要与指定机构合作完成相关程序。

Farm Stream

农场主类别
Farm Stream

The Farm Stream is intended for experienced farm owners and operators who have the financial resources and management experience to establish or purchase a farming business in Alberta.

适用于具有农场经营管理经验、 资金能力并计划在阿省 建立或购买农业企业的申请人。

AAIP现在不能只看EOI分数

AAIP目前使用Worker Expression of Interest体系， 但EOI分数并不是省政府选择候选人的唯一因素。

阿省还会结合本省经济发展、 劳动力市场需求、重点行业、 职业、阿省就业情况 以及年度省提名名额进行选择。

近年来医疗、科技、建筑、 农业、制造业、航空 及部分技工职业持续受到关注。

因此，申请人即使达到某次抽选的最低分数， 也不能简单理解为一定会在下一轮收到邀请。 实际竞争力需要结合 Stream、EOI、职业、行业、 Job Offer、雇主及当前选择方向综合判断。
