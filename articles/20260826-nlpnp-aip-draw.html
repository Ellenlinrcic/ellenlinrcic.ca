纽芬兰与拉布拉多省再次发出140份移民邀请

Read in English

纽芬兰与拉布拉多省于2026年8月26日进行了 新一轮经济移民邀请， 共发出140份Invitation to Apply（ITA）。

本轮邀请分配：

  • NLPNP： 94份
  • Atlantic Immigration Program（AIP）： 46份

这是8月份第三轮邀请

值得注意的是， 这是纽芬兰与拉布拉多省 在2026年8月份进行的第三轮经济移民邀请。

8月10日， 该省共发出208份邀请， 其中186份属于NLPNP， 22份属于AIP。

8月18日， 该省又发出122份邀请， 全部属于NLPNP。

到了8月26日， AIP再次获得46个邀请名额。

日期 NLPNP AIP 总邀请数
2026年8月10日 186 22 208
2026年8月18日 122 0 122
2026年8月26日 94 46 140

今年抽选节奏明显加快

纽芬兰与拉布拉多省自2025年开始采用 Expression of Interest（EOI） 模式管理经济类移民申请。

进入2026年后， 该省持续通过批次方式发出邀请。

仅在2026年8月份， 就已经进行了三轮邀请。

这种较为频繁的邀请节奏， 对已经具备当地工作机会 或符合省级劳动力市场需求的申请人来说， 值得持续关注。

纽芬兰与拉布拉多省没有公布最低EOI分数

与Express Entry等打分制项目不同， 纽芬兰与拉布拉多省公布的抽选结果 并没有提供最低EOI分数， 也没有公布完整的职业名单。

因此， 申请人不能简单通过 “多少分可以被邀请” 来判断自己的机会。

对申请人而言， 更重要的是自己的职业、 当地就业情况、雇主， 以及申请是否符合 纽芬兰与拉布拉多省当前的 劳动力市场优先方向。

有当地Job Offer，NLPNP和AIP值得同时评估

对已经获得纽芬兰与拉布拉多省雇主 Job Offer的外国工人来说， NLPNP和Atlantic Immigration Program 都可能值得进一步评估。

两个项目虽然都可能帮助申请人 取得加拿大永久居民身份， 但申请条件、雇主要求和申请路径并不完全相同。

因此， 在制定移民策略的时候， 不应该只关注其中一个项目。

更合理的做法是结合申请人的职业、 工作经验、当地Job Offer、 雇主情况及整体移民背景， 比较NLPNP和AIP 是否分别存在适用机会。

Newfoundland and Labrador Issues 140 Immigration Invitations in Latest Draw

Newfoundland and Labrador conducted another economic immigration selection round on August 26, 2026, issuing a total of 140 Invitations to Apply (ITAs).

The invitations were divided as follows:

  • Newfoundland and Labrador Provincial Nominee Program (NLPNP): 94
  • Atlantic Immigration Program (AIP): 46

Third Invitation Round in August

This was already the province’s third invitation round in August.

On August 10, Newfoundland and Labrador issued 208 invitations, including 186 under the NLPNP and 22 under the AIP.

Another 122 invitations were issued on August 18, all under the NLPNP.

The August 26 selection brought the AIP back into the province’s latest invitation round, with 46 AIP invitations issued.

Date NLPNP AIP Total
August 10, 2026 186 22 208
August 18, 2026 122 0 122
August 26, 2026 94 46 140

Newfoundland and Labrador Continues Frequent Selections

Newfoundland and Labrador introduced an Expression of Interest (EOI) model for its economic immigration programs in 2025.

The province has continued to issue invitations in batches throughout 2026, with three selection rounds taking place in August alone.

No Published Minimum EOI Score

Unlike some points-based immigration programs, Newfoundland and Labrador does not publish a minimum EOI score or a complete list of occupations selected in each round.

Applicants therefore should not assess their chances based on a single expected “cut-off score.”

Occupation, employment in the province, the employer, and alignment with Newfoundland and Labrador’s current labour market priorities can all affect an applicant’s prospects.

NLPNP and AIP May Both Be Worth Assessing

For foreign workers who already have a job offer from a Newfoundland and Labrador employer, both the NLPNP and the Atlantic Immigration Program may be worth assessing when developing an immigration strategy.

The two programs have different eligibility requirements, employer requirements and application processes.

Applicants should therefore consider their occupation, work experience, job offer, employer and overall immigration circumstances when determining which pathway may be appropriate.
