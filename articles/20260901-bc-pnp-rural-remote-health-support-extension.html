BC省提名农村及偏远地区医疗支持项目 延长注册至10月7日

Read in English

BC省提名计划（BC PNP）已将 Temporary Rural/Remote Health Support Initiative （农村及偏远地区医疗支持临时项目） 的注册期限延长至 2026年10月7日晚上11:59（BC时间）。

该项目原定于2026年8月31日结束注册。 BC PNP于8月13日宣布延期， 主要是为了照顾可能受到山火影响的申请人。

此次延期也为已经符合资格、 但仍在等待卫生局内部审核、 雇主支持或完成注册准备的申请人， 提供了额外时间。

这是一个什么项目？

Temporary Rural/Remote Health Support Initiative 是BC PNP推出的一项一次性临时措施， 面向已经在BC省农村或偏远地区 为公共卫生局工作的部分清洁及保安人员。

BC省计划通过这一项目 最多提名250人。

2026年7月23日， BC PNP已经举行首轮相关抽选， 共发出60份申请邀请， 最低注册分数为50分。

哪些申请人可能符合条件？

申请人需要满足BC PNP的一般要求， 同时符合这项临时措施的特别条件。

  • 必须是符合条件的BC公共卫生局的 直接雇员， 而不是承包商或外包公司的员工；
  • 必须从事以下三个指定职业之一：
  • NOC 64410： Security guards and related security service occupations；
  • NOC 65310： Light duty cleaners；
  • NOC 65312： Janitors, caretakers and heavy-duty cleaners；
  • 必须从事长期、没有固定终止日期的全职工作；
  • 工作地点必须位于符合项目定义的 农村或偏远地区；
  • 注册前必须已经为同一卫生局雇主， 在合资格地点连续全职工作 至少9个月；
  • 必须取得卫生局雇主的正式支持；
  • 在注册及申请审理期间， 通常需要继续为同一卫生局、 在同一合资格地点和职业中全职工作；
  • 至少完成高中教育， 并满足适用的语言、收入及其他BC PNP要求。

哪些公共卫生局符合条件？

目前符合条件的公共卫生局包括：

  • Provincial Health Services Authority；
  • First Nations Health Authority；
  • Fraser Health；
  • Interior Health；
  • Island Health；
  • Northern Health；
  • Vancouver Coastal Health；
  • Providence Health Care。

延期是否意味着申请条件放宽？

不是。

此次调整只延长了注册截止日期， 并没有取消或放宽项目的核心资格条件。

申请人仍然需要满足：

  • 9个月连续全职工作经验；
  • 必须由符合条件的卫生局直接雇佣；
  • 必须属于指定清洁或保安职业；
  • 必须在合资格的农村或偏远地区工作；
  • 必须获得卫生局雇主支持。

完成注册并不等于已经获得省提名。 申请人仍需进入注册池， 等待BC PNP发出邀请， 并在获邀后递交完整申请和证明文件。

此次延期有什么实际意义？

此次延期最有价值的地方， 是让已经满足条件， 或正在等待卫生局内部审核和支持程序的员工， 获得更多准备时间。

需要特别注意的是， 卫生局并没有义务支持每一名员工， 而且不同卫生局可能设有自己的内部审核程序。

如果申请人已经接近或达到项目要求， 不建议等到10月截止日期前才开始准备。 应尽早确认自己的职业、工作地点、 9个月工作经验以及雇主支持情况。

BC PNP Extends Rural and Remote Health Support Initiative Registration to October 7, 2026

The BC Provincial Nominee Program has extended the registration deadline for its Temporary Rural/Remote Health Support Initiative to 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on October 7, 2026.

Registration was originally scheduled to close on August 31, 2026. BC PNP announced the extension on August 13, primarily to accommodate applicants who may have been affected by wildfires.

The additional time may also benefit eligible health authority employees who are still completing their employer support or registration process.

What Is the Temporary Rural/Remote Health Support Initiative?

This is a temporary, one-time BC PNP initiative designed for certain cleaning and security workers who are already employed by public health authorities in rural or remote communities in British Columbia.

BC plans to nominate up to 250 people through the initiative.

The first invitation round took place on July 23, 2026, when BC PNP issued 60 invitations to apply, with a minimum registration score of 50.

Who May Qualify?

Applicants must meet the general BC PNP requirements as well as the special conditions of this temporary initiative.

  • The applicant must be directly employed by an eligible BC public health authority;
  • The applicant must work in one of the following occupations:
  • NOC 64410 – Security guards and related security service occupations;
  • NOC 65310 – Light duty cleaners;
  • NOC 65312 – Janitors, caretakers and heavy-duty cleaners;
  • The position must be full-time and indeterminate;
  • The applicant must work at an eligible rural or remote location;
  • Before registering, the applicant must have completed at least nine consecutive months of full-time employment with the same health authority at an eligible location;
  • Formal employer support is required;
  • The applicant must generally continue working in the same qualifying position while the registration and application are being processed;
  • The applicant must have completed at least secondary school and meet applicable language, income and other BC PNP requirements.

Which Health Authorities Are Eligible?

  • Provincial Health Services Authority;
  • First Nations Health Authority;
  • Fraser Health;
  • Interior Health;
  • Island Health;
  • Northern Health;
  • Vancouver Coastal Health; and
  • Providence Health Care.

Does the Extension Change the Eligibility Requirements?

No.

The extension only changes the deadline for registration. It does not remove or relax the core eligibility requirements.

Applicants must still meet requirements including nine months of qualifying employment, direct employment by an eligible health authority, an eligible occupation and work location, and employer support.

Registration also does not guarantee a provincial nomination. Eligible applicants must first enter the registration pool, receive an invitation from BC PNP, and then submit a complete application with supporting documents.

What Does the Extension Mean for Potential Applicants?

The extension is particularly useful for employees who already meet the eligibility requirements but are still waiting for their health authority to complete its internal review or employer support process.

Health authorities are not required to support every employee, and individual health authorities may have their own internal procedures for assessing requests.

Eligible workers considering this pathway should therefore begin the employer support and registration process as soon as possible rather than waiting until the October deadline.

Registration deadline: October 7, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time.
